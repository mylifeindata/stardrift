Stardrift — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

The short version: Stardrift does not collect, store, or share any of your data.

What the app stores

Your game progress — mission completion, high scores, saved games, and settings — is stored only on your device, using local storage inside the app. It never leaves your device, and we have no servers and no way to see it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, Stardrift submits your scores to Game Center leaderboards so you can compare them with other players. Game Center is a service operated by Apple Inc. and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Stardrift receives no personal information from Game Center and does not track your identity.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No accounts, sign-ups, or email collection
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No data sold or shared with anyone, because none is collected

Children

Stardrift is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data, no personal information is collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes how the app handles data, this page will be updated first and the change will be described in the App Store release notes.
