Stardrift: Merge the Cosmos — Support

Stardrift is a space-themed sliding-merge puzzle: 30 hand-crafted missions, four endless Free Play boards, and Game Center leaderboards.

FAQ

My progress disappeared. Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes its save data; reinstalling starts fresh.

Scores aren't appearing on leaderboards. Make sure you're signed in to Game Center (Settings → Game Center) and that the app has been opened at least once since signing in.

How do anchors work? Anchors never slide, but a tile of equal value merges into them and doubles them in place. Some missions require growing anchors to a target value.
